# Market Dynamics, TAM Sizing & Why Now (Timing)

## Market Sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM) Pitfalls
1. **Top-Down Illusion**: "The healthcare market is $4 Trillion, if we get 1%, we are a $40B company." (Fatal flaw: ignores unit transaction volume and real willing buyers).
2. **Bottom-Up Reality**: TAM = Total addressable target accounts × Annual Contract Value (ACV) or Annual Willingness to Pay.

## "Why Now?" Catalysts
- **Technological Shift**: A new API, platform, or capability enables what was impossible 24 months ago.
- **Regulatory Change**: New laws mandate or deregulate industry compliance.
- **Behavioral Inflection**: Consumer/Enterprise habits have shifted permanently (e.g., remote work, AI adoption).

## Go-To-Market (GTM) Friction
- Enterprise sales cycles: 6 to 12 months minimum.
- "Nice-to-have" vitamins vs "Must-have" painkillers during economic downturns.
