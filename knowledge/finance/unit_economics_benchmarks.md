# Financial Analysis & Unit Economics Benchmarks

## Golden Rules of Venture Financials
1. **LTV / CAC Ratio**:
   - Healthy SaaS/Marketplace: > 3.0x.
   - Danger zone: < 2.0x (company spends more on acquisition than customer lifetime value).
   - CAC Payback Period: Ideally under 12 months for SMB SaaS, under 18 months for Enterprise.

2. **Gross Margin Realities**:
   - Pure B2B Software / SaaS: 75% - 85%+.
   - AI-wrapper / Inference-heavy products: 50% - 65% (severely degraded by GPU cloud inference costs).
   - Marketplace / FinTech Take-Rate: 15% - 25% take rate, 60% gross margin.
   - Hardware / Logistics: 25% - 40%.

3. **Burn Multiple & Efficiency**:
   - Burn Multiple = Net Burn / Net New ARR.
   - Outstanding: < 1.0x (burning $1 to generate >$1 ARR).
   - Poor: > 2.0x (burning $2+ for every $1 of ARR added).

4. **Pricing Power & Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**:
   - Top-quartile B2B SaaS: > 120% NRR.
   - High customer churn (> 3% monthly) invalidates long-term LTV calculations.
