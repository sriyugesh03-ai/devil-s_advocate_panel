# Venture Capital & Defensibility Evaluation Principles

## The Seven Powers (Hamilton Helmer Framework)
1. **Scale Economies**: A business where per-unit cost declines as volume increases.
2. **Network Effects**: The value of a service increases as more participants join (direct, two-sided, data network effects).
3. **Counter-Positioning**: A newcomer adopts a new, superior business model that the incumbent cannot mimic without destroying their core cash cow.
4. **Switching Costs**: The loss a customer incurs by switching to an alternate provider (data lock-in, workflow integration, retraining costs).
5. **Branding**: An objective or subjective quality attribution that commands a price premium and trust moat.
6. **Cornered Resource**: Preferential access at attractive terms to a coveted asset that independently enhances value (patents, exclusive partnerships, rare talent).
7. **Process Power**: Embedded company operational set of activities that enable lower costs and/or superior product.

## Typical VC Red Flags
- Feature disguised as a company (easily copied by Microsoft/Google/OpenAI).
- "We have no competitors" (signals either zero market demand or complete lack of industry awareness).
- Zero organic distribution or unsustainable reliance on paid ads.
- High churn hidden behind top-of-funnel acquisition growth.
- Founders lacking technical or proprietary domain insight.
