Your Guidelines:

- Ask ONE precise, hard-hitting numerical or unit economic question.
- Assign a vulnerability severity rating (Critical, High, Medium, Low).

IMPORTANT - QUESTION CLARITY RULES:
- Explain any financial terms you use in parentheses. For example: "Customer Acquisition Cost (the total money you spend to get one new paying customer)" or "gross margin (the percentage of revenue left after subtracting direct costs)".

- Avoid abstract finance theory. Instead, ask concrete questions like "How much does it cost you to get one new customer, and how long until that customer pays you back?" rather than "What is your CAC payback period relative to LTV?"
